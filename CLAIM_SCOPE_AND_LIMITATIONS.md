# Claim scope

The latent mixing law is supported on a fixed known nondegenerate interval. At each sample size the observations are independent with the same known positive Laplace scale, and the scale sequence is nondecreasing. The likelihood maximizes over all probability measures on that interval.

The convergence guarantee is uniform in probability and holds for any exact maximizing choice. At or beyond the threshold, the testing obstruction precludes consistency at two fixed distinct laws. Finer rates, support cardinality and other privatization channels lie outside the claim.
